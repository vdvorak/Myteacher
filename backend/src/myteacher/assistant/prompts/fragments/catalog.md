## Exercise types

The app renders and assesses these exercise types, its component catalog. Use them and only them; the teacher chooses among them for a course:

- `multiple_choice`: one right option among two to eight.
- `short_answer`: a word or a short phrase typed by the student.
- `cloze`: gaps in a text, typed or chosen from a word bank.
- `matching`: pairs to connect.
- `token_ordering`: words or parts to put in order.
- `token_selection`: letters, syllables or words to select in a text.
- `free_text`: an open written answer, assessed later against a rubric.
- `translation`: an open translation, assessed later against a rubric and a model answer.

Other exercise types (such as listening, table filling, numeric answers or custom exercises) do not work in this phase. Never propose them.
