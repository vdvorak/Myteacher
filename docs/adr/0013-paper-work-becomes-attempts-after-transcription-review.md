# Paper work becomes attempts, but only after the teacher reviews the transcription

Teachers still give tests on paper and spend their evenings marking them. We decided that a classroom material can be handed out as a **paper sitting**: each student gets a personal printed copy with their name and a QR code. The teacher scans the whole stack. The assistant reads the handwriting through the teacher's provider key. Each student's sheets then become an ordinary **attempt** on that material, so assessment, overrides, published results, grading and concept states work as they do for work done in the app. The assistant's reading of the handwriting is not trusted on its own. The teacher confirms it beside the scan in a **transcription review** before anything is assessed. A misread answer would otherwise turn into a wrong mark and a wrong concept state without anyone noticing.

Paper sittings are for materials made of questions. The app assesses answers to questions, not the content and quality of a free piece of writing, so essays are out of scope and answer length limits stay as they are.

## Considered options

- **Assess the scan directly, with no transcription step**: rejected, because the teacher could not see what the assistant believed the student wrote, and an override would have nothing to correct against.
- **A separate "paper results" record beside attempts**: rejected, because it would duplicate assessment, publishing, grading and concept states for no gain.
- **Students photograph their own sheets**: kept for later, and only for material released in the app. There the student reviews the transcription themselves, because it is their own submission. For a stack scanned by the teacher, the teacher reviews it.

## Consequences

- Scans of students' handwriting, possibly minors' (ADR 0007), are sent to the teacher's AI provider as the scans of sources already are. They are kept with the attempt until the run is ended, at most 90 days, as a record for disputes, and they are removed by erasure.
- An attempt no longer always starts from a release. A student may have attempts from both paper and the app on one material in one run; only one counts, and the teacher retracts the other.
- Pages are matched to students by the printed QR code. A page without a readable code falls back to the name written on it, and the teacher confirms every uncertain page.
